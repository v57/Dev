Dev is a toolbox for people who make apps on the Mac. Open an Xcode project and it gets its own tabs:

- Localization: translate your string catalog with the model you choose, using your own key.
- App Icon: an Icon Composer icon from flat sources, one background and one glyph color.
- Screenshots: framed App Store pictures with translated captions.
- Workflows: builds and releases on this Mac or its virtual machines, with no server.
- Store Text: the name, description and release notes of every store, kept as text files in your project.

Tools without a project: Storage (a disk cleaner and app uninstaller), Virtual Machines (UTM), and Localization Servers.

Agents can reach it through `Dev --mcp`, with permissions you set.

Needs macOS 15 or later on Apple silicon. MIT license.
